This lesson is a tour of the parts inside a radio: batteries, capacitors, resistors, diodes, transistors and even vacuum tubes. For each one, the exam wants a single defining fact.

Batteries

  • A 12-volt lead-acid battery lasts longest if you never discharge it below about 10.5 volts.
  • Low internal resistance lets a battery deliver high discharge current. That matters for a transmitter, which draws a lot while you talk.

Capacitors and resistors

PartThe fact to know
Electrolytic capacitorHigh capacitance for its size
Low-voltage ceramic capacitorLow cost
Wire-wound resistorDon't use it in RF circuits: it's a coil of wire, and its inductance makes the circuit unpredictable

Every real inductor also has a little stray capacitance between its turns. Above its self-resonant frequency, an inductor acts like a capacitor.

Diodes

A diode doesn't conduct until the forward voltage reaches a threshold:

  • Silicon: about 0.7 V
  • Germanium: about 0.3 V

Transistors

  • A bipolar transistor used as a switch flips between two states: saturation (fully on) and cutoff (fully off). The in-between "active region" is for amplifiers.
  • In a MOSFET, the gate is separated from the channel by a thin insulating layer. That's the "oxide" in metal-oxide-semiconductor. Because of it, the gate draws almost no current.

Vacuum tubes

Tubes still turn up in high-power amplifiers. Electrons flow from the cathode to the plate.

  • The control grid regulates that flow, like a tap.
  • The screen grid's main job is to reduce grid-to-plate capacitance, which could otherwise cause unwanted feedback.

Key facts

  • 12 V lead-acid: don't go below 10.5 V. Low internal resistance: high discharge current.
  • Electrolytic: high capacitance per volume. Ceramic: low cost.
  • No wire-wound resistors at RF: their inductance.
  • Inductor above self-resonance: becomes capacitive.
  • Diode threshold: silicon 0.7 V, germanium 0.3 V.
  • Transistor switch: saturation and cutoff. MOSFET: gate insulated from the channel.
  • Tube: control grid regulates the electrons; screen grid reduces grid-to-plate capacitance.

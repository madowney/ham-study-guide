A schematic is a circuit diagram drawn with standard symbols. The exam shows three figures and asks you to name the numbered parts, so this lesson walks through each one.

What schematics show

A schematic accurately shows how components are connected. It doesn't show wire lengths or what the parts physically look like.

#ComponentHow to recognize it

An arrow through or touching a symbol means "adjustable": a variable capacitor, a variable resistor, a variable inductor.

Key facts

  • Schematic = a diagram with standard symbols. It shows connections, not lengths or looks.
  • T-1: 1 resistor, 2 transistor (controls current), 3 lamp, 4 battery.
  • T-2: 4 transformer, 6 capacitor, 8 LED, 9 variable resistor (3 is an SPST switch).
  • T-3: 3 variable inductor, 4 antenna.
